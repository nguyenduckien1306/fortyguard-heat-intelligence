## ✨ Main Features

### 🗺️ Smart Heat Maps
The app creates colorful maps of your city where hot areas glow orange and red. Cool areas appear blue or green. You can zoom in,pan around, and see the heat right on the streets.



### ⚠️ Automatic Alerts
Set a temperature limit (for example: "alert me if it gets above  ution95°F"). The app watches heat levels everywhere and instantly notifies you when any spot crosses that line. No need to keep checking the map yourself.



### 🏆 Priority Score (Who Needs Help First)
Not all hot spots are equally urgent. The app gives each location a number score based on:
- How hot it is
- How many people are nearby
- Whether sensitive places (hospitals, schools) are affected

Higher score = act first. This helps you use your time and resources wisely.



### 🔍 Investigation Queue
Imagine a to-do list of heat problems automatically created for you. Each item tells you what's happening, where it is, how serious it is, and one click takes you straight to that spot on the map. You can mark items as "done", add notes, and keep a record of whatyou did.



### 🔒 Proof & Evidence Vault
Every piece of information in this app comes with a secure digital fingerprint. This means if someone asks: "How do you know it was hot there?", you can prove it with unchangeable, tamper-proof records. Great for official reports or transparency.



### 📊 Simple Charts & Trends
See how heat changes over hours, days, and weeks. The app draws easy-to-read graphs that show whether things are getting better or worse.
